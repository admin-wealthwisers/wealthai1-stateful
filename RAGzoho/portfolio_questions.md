# Portfolio Information Questions for Clients

## Basic Portfolio Information

### Overall Portfolio Overview
1. What is your total portfolio value (AUM)?
2. How would you describe your investment portfolio composition?
3. What percentage of your portfolio is allocated to different asset classes?
4. What is your current investment strategy (conservative, moderate, aggressive)?
5. How many investment accounts do you currently hold?

### Asset Allocation
6. What percentage of your portfolio is in equities/stocks?
7. What percentage is in fixed income/bonds?
8. What percentage is in mutual funds?
9. Do you have any alternative investments (real estate, commodities, etc.)?
10. What percentage is held in cash or cash equivalents?

### Investment Products
11. Which mutual funds are you currently invested in?
12. What are your SIP (Systematic Investment Plan) details?
   - SIP top-up date
   - SIP top-up amount
   - SIP alarm date
13. Do you hold any direct stocks?
14. Are you invested in any ETFs (Exchange Traded Funds)?
15. Do you have any ULIPs or insurance-linked investments?
16. Are you invested in any PMS (Portfolio Management Services)?

### Portfolio Holdings
17. What are your top 5 holdings by value?
18. What is your largest investment position?
19. Which sectors or industries do you have exposure to?
20. Do you have any international/exposure to foreign markets?
21. What percentage of your portfolio is in large-cap, mid-cap, and small-cap stocks?

### Performance & Returns
22. What is your portfolio's current return/yield?
23. What is your investment time horizon?
24. What are your target returns/expectations?
25. How has your portfolio performed over the last year?
26. What is your risk-adjusted return expectation?

### Risk Profile
27. What is your risk tolerance level?
28. How comfortable are you with market volatility?
29. What is the maximum loss you can tolerate?
30. Are there any sectors or investments you want to avoid?

### Goals & Objectives
31. What are your primary investment goals?
32. What are your short-term financial goals (1-3 years)?
33. What are your long-term financial goals (5+ years)?
34. Do you have any specific life events you're saving for (retirement, education, etc.)?
35. When do you plan to withdraw or need these funds?

### Active Management
36. How actively do you manage your portfolio?
37. Do you rebalance your portfolio regularly?
38. When was the last time you reviewed your portfolio?
39. Do you prefer active or passive investment management?
40. Are you open to portfolio restructuring or rebalancing?

### Tax Considerations
41. What is your current tax bracket?
42. Are you using any tax-saving investment options (ELSS, PPF, etc.)?
43. What is your tax liability from investments?
44. Are you aware of capital gains tax implications?
45. Do you need tax-efficient investment strategies?

### Liquidity Needs
46. What percentage of your portfolio do you need to keep liquid?
47. Do you have an emergency fund separate from investments?
48. How quickly might you need to access your invested funds?
49. Do you have any upcoming major expenses?
50. What is your cash flow requirement from investments?

### Portfolio Details for Specific Clients
51. What is your account number for portfolio tracking?
52. Which broker or investment platform do you use?
53. Do you have multiple Demat accounts?
54. What is your folio number for mutual fund investments?
55. Do you have nominee details updated for all investments?

### Investment Preferences
56. Are you interested in ESG (Environmental, Social, Governance) investing?
57. Do you prefer dividend-paying investments or growth stocks?
58. Are you open to new investment opportunities?
59. What is your opinion on market timing?
60. Do you follow any specific investment philosophy or approach?

### Reporting & Communication
61. How often would you like portfolio reports?
62. What format do you prefer for portfolio updates (email, portal, physical)?
63. Do you want real-time notifications about portfolio changes?
64. What level of detail do you want in portfolio statements?
65. Do you track your portfolio performance yourself?

## Follow-up Questions Based on Responses

### If Client Mentions Mutual Funds
- What is the breakup between equity, debt, and hybrid funds?
- Are you invested in any sector-specific or thematic funds?
- Do you have SIPs running or lump sum investments?

### If Client Mentions Stocks
- What is your stock selection criteria?
- Do you use fundamental or technical analysis?
- How many individual stocks do you hold?

### If Client Mentions Low Portfolio Value
- What is preventing you from investing more?
- Would you like to increase your investment amount gradually?
- Are there any financial constraints we should consider?

### If Client Mentions High Portfolio Value
- How do you manage diversification across accounts?
- Do you need advanced portfolio management strategies?
- Are there estate planning considerations?

## Sample Conversation Starters

**Casual Approach:**
- "I'd like to understand your current investment portfolio better. Could you share some details?"
- "To provide you with the best advice, could you tell me about your existing investments?"

**Structured Approach:**
- "Let's review your portfolio. What is your current total investment value and allocation?"
- "I'd like to conduct a portfolio health check. Can you share your investment details?"

**Goal-Oriented Approach:**
- "To help you achieve your financial goals, I need to understand your current portfolio. What investments do you have?"
- "What does your investment portfolio look like, and how does it align with your financial objectives?"

## Questions for Portfolio Updates

66. Have you made any new investments recently?
67. Have you redeemed or sold any investments recently?
68. Have there been any changes to your SIP amounts?
69. Has your risk profile changed recently?
70. Have there been any major life changes affecting your investment goals?

## Questions for Portfolio Analysis

71. Are you satisfied with your current portfolio performance?
72. Are there any investments you're concerned about?
73. Do you feel your portfolio is properly diversified?
74. Are you missing any important asset classes?
75. Would you like professional portfolio analysis and recommendations?

---

## How to Use These Questions

1. **Initial Client Onboarding**: Use questions 1-25 to get basic portfolio information
2. **Deep Dive**: Use questions 26-50 for detailed portfolio analysis
3. **Regular Updates**: Use questions 66-70 for periodic portfolio reviews
4. **Problem Solving**: Use follow-up questions based on client responses
5. **Customization**: Adapt questions based on client's investment experience level

**Note**: Always explain why you're asking each question and how the information will help you serve them better.
