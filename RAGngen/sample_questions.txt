Sample Questions for NGEN Markets Mutual Fund Data
====================================================

FILTER RESULTS & SCREENING:
- What are the top performing mutual funds based on the filter results?
- Show me mutual funds with the highest returns in the filter results
- Which funds passed the NGEN Markets filter criteria?
- What are the filter parameters used in NGEN Markets Filter Results?
- List all mutual funds that meet the minimum investment criteria

MUTUAL FUND CATEGORY AVERAGES:
- What is the average return for large-cap equity funds?
- Compare category averages between equity and debt mutual funds
- Which mutual fund category has the highest average return?
- What are the average expense ratios across different fund categories?
- Show me the category-wise average AUM (Assets Under Management)
- What is the average 1-year, 3-year, and 5-year return for mid-cap funds?
- Compare average returns between growth and value fund categories

AMCs (ASSET MANAGEMENT COMPANIES):
- List all AMCs in the NGEN Markets database
- Which AMC has the highest total AUM?
- Show me the number of funds managed by each AMC
- What is the average fund performance by AMC?
- Which AMC has the best performing equity funds?
- Compare AMCs based on their fund count and average returns

EQUITY EXPOSURES:
- What are the top sector exposures in mutual fund portfolios?
- Which stocks have the highest allocation in equity mutual funds?
- Show me the sector-wise distribution of equity exposures
- What percentage of equity funds are invested in technology stocks?
- List the top 10 stocks by market capitalization in equity fund holdings
- What is the average equity exposure percentage across all funds?
- Which sectors are most overweight or underweight in mutual fund portfolios?

TOP 25 STOCKS:
- What are the top 25 stocks held by mutual funds?
- Which stock has the highest total holding value across all mutual funds?
- Show me the percentage allocation of each top 25 stock
- How many mutual funds hold each of the top 25 stocks?
- What is the combined market value of top 25 stock holdings?
- Which sectors do the top 25 stocks belong to?
- Compare the holdings percentage of top 5 stocks vs bottom 5 in the top 25 list

DEBT EXPOSURES:
- What are the top debt instruments held by mutual funds?
- Show me the credit rating distribution of debt holdings
- What percentage of debt funds are invested in government securities?
- List the top corporate bonds held by debt mutual funds
- What is the average maturity profile of debt fund holdings?
- Which debt fund categories have the highest average yields?
- Compare AAA, AA, and A rated bond exposures in debt funds

NFOs (NEW FUND OFFERS):
- List all recent NFOs from NGEN Markets
- What are the upcoming NFO launch dates?
- Show me NFOs by fund category (equity, debt, hybrid)
- What is the minimum investment amount for recent NFOs?
- Which AMCs have launched the most NFOs recently?
- What are the investment objectives of recent NFOs?
- Compare NFO sizes (fund collection targets) across different categories

CROSS-CATEGORY QUESTIONS:
- Compare equity vs debt fund performance and exposures
- Which AMC has the best track record across equity and debt categories?
- What is the overall mutual fund industry trend based on all data?
- Show me funds that appear in both filter results and top performers
- What is the correlation between fund size and performance?
- Which stocks appear in both equity exposures and top 25 holdings?
- Compare category averages with individual fund performances in filter results
